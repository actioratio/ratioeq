This policy applies to the Ratio EQ Android app and its legal and support pages.

Data used by the app

Ratio EQ works offline. The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not send app data to us or to other recipients.

When you create an EQ profile, the app stores its name and seven band levels. When you assign a profile, it stores the device name and the Bluetooth address reported by Android to recognise the device when it reconnects. The app also stores your default profile and on/off setting.

Access and permissions

Ratio EQ reads the media output selected by Android so it can load the matching profile. The optional Nearby devices permission lets the app list additional connected Bluetooth audio devices for profile assignment. It does not request location or microphone access or record audio. Android modifies the sound through its audio effect.

The foreground service keeps the enabled equalizer and automatic profile switching running when the app is closed. If you allow notifications, you can see the ongoing service and turn it off there. After a restart, the enabled state is restored the first time you unlock your device. You can revoke optional permissions in Android settings.

Purpose, legal basis and deletion

Where local device or profile information is personal data, the app processes it to provide the features you choose under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Android keeps it in the app’s private storage. Ratio EQ excludes it from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

You can remove individual profiles and device assignments in the app. To remove all data, clear Ratio EQ’s storage in Android app settings or uninstall the app.

Google Play and support

Google Play handles downloads, payments and refund requests. The app does not request payment or Google account details. Google’s privacy policy covers its own processing. Where Google provides order or billing data, such as an order reference, purchase date, country or amount, we use it to fulfil purchases and process refunds under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and to comply with accounting and retention obligations under Article 6(1)(c). Retention follows those obligations.

If you contact us by email, we process your contact details, message and any attachments you choose to send as needed to handle your request. We rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual or support requests, otherwise on our legitimate interest in answering under Article 6(1)(f), and on Article 6(1)(c) where required by law. We may use email and IT providers. We delete requests after resolving them when legal retention duties and any need to retain them for legal claims have ended.

Support pages and your rights

GitHub Pages hosts the public pages. When you visit, GitHub processes technical access data, such as your IP address, to serve the pages and keep the service secure. We do not set our own cookies or use analytics scripts on these pages. We operate the pages under our legitimate interest in accessible product information under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub may process data in the United States. Its linked privacy statement explains retention and the safeguards for transfers.

Subject to the GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can change or delete your local app settings on your device. Contact us at office@actioratio.at if you have questions or requests. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
