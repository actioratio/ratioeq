Purchase and support – Ratio EQ

Completely ad-free

Ratio EQ is a 7-band equalizer for Android 13 and later. Save your EQ settings as profiles and assign them to Bluetooth audio devices. Android chooses the media output. When switched on, the equalizer works while the app is closed and restarts after you first unlock your device following a reboot. The effect depends on the device, media output and support in the playback path.

One-time purchase through Google Play

You can use the equalizer free for seven days after your first launch. Then the equalizer pauses until you unlock it permanently with a one-time in-app purchase. The trial ends without an automatic charge. You can restore access with the Google Play account used for the purchase. Google Play shows the final price and the applicable contract and withdrawal information before you complete your purchase.

Refunds, withdrawal and defects

For refund requests or withdrawal information, open Google Play Help for your purchase. You can also email us about a defect or purchase or to give notice of withdrawal. Include the Google Play order reference if available. Your statutory consumer and warranty rights are unaffected.

Help and security reports

For help or confidential security reports, contact office@actioratio.at. For bugs, please include the app version, Android device model and a brief description.
